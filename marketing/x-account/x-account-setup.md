## Steps

1. Go to **x.com** on your phone or computer and tap **Create account**. Use an email you check (a new Gmail like spinxigame@gmail.com keeps it separate from your personal account) and your phone number for the code.
2. When X asks for a username, type the first handle from the list above. X tells you straight away if it's taken.
3. Go to your profile, tap **Edit profile** and add:
   - the header photo: `cover-photo-1500x500.png`
   - the profile photo: `profile-picture-400.png`
   - the name, bio and website from above
   Then tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Post**, attach the four how-to images in order, paste the pinned post text, add the alt text, and tap **Post**.
5. On that post, tap the **···** menu and choose **Pin to your profile**.
6. Follow a few big FPL accounts (the official @OfficialFPL and Fantasy Football Scout, @FFScout) so X starts showing you, and suggesting you to, the FPL crowd.
