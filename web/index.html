How to play

1Spin & pick your XI

  1. Hit Play on the next gameweek and choose a formation.
  2. SPIN the reel — it lands on a random team. Teams can come up more than once.
  3. Pick any player from that team — they fill the next open slot for their position (GK / DEF / MID / FWD). Each player can only be picked once.
  4. Nobody fits your remaining slots? Spin again.
  5. Repeat until your XI is complete.

2Captain & enter

  1. Tap a player on the pitch to make them captain — they score double points (negatives too!). You can change your mind until you enter the team.
  2. Enter my Team saves it and posts it to the Leaderboard under your name (set your name on the Leaderboard tab first).
  3. One team per gameweek: your first team is the one that counts. Any more are practice. Entries close at the gameweek's first kick-off.
  4. Enter early, earn a re-spin. If your team is in at least 12 hours before the deadline, you can re-spin once, any time before kick-off: tap one player to drop, spin, and pick a replacement from the club it lands on. Handy when the team news comes out.
  5. On the Season board, your team in each gameweek counts towards your total.
  6. Points appear as the real matches finish — a live tag means games are still to be played and the score will keep updating.
