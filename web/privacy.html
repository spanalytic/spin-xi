Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Spin XI is a free Premier League wheel game at spinxi.app, also available as an Android app. This page explains what it stores about you. The Android app is the same site, so everything here applies to both.

What we store

You can play without signing in; practice teams then stay only on your device. If you sign in with Google or an emailed link, we store:

  • your name, as shown on the leaderboard;
  • your email address, used only to sign you in (and to email you, if you've ticked the box to get emails);
  • the teams you enter and when you entered them.

Your name and teams are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Where it's kept

Sign-in and teams are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, stored in London). Your browser also keeps a few settings and your practice teams in local storage on your device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Player scores come from the official Fantasy Premier League data.

What we don't do

There are no ads and no tracking or analytics, and we never sell or share your information with anyone else.

Emails

Emails are opt-in. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can turn them off at any time.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and everything stored about you, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Spin XI account". Your sign-in, name, email address and teams are deleted within 30 days, and your teams are removed from the leaderboard.
